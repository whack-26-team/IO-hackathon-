REMY: two directions, v2

Built from real kitchen things and a real character, instead of generic app cards and emoji.

A · "The Pass": the restaurant ticket rail

Remy's comments print out like order tickets on thermal paper, timers are tickets clipped to a steel rail, and steps are label-maker tape. A safety alert is a red kitchen stamp. Paper cream, ink black, one tomato red, typewriter type and condensed headlines. Feels like a real kitchen, not an app.

B · "Remy": a drawn character

A drawn chef face, not emoji, whose eyebrows and mouth change with the moment. Flat colours, thick ink outlines and hard offset shadows, like a sticker. The timer is a kitchen dial. Playful, but with a real visual identity Felix can build in SVG.
